The Unique Biodiversity

Mount Malindang is recognized as one of the most significant conservation sites in the Philippines, hosting a rich array of endemic flora and fauna that thrive in its diverse elevations and climates.

High Endemism

A large percentage of species found here are unique to the Philippines or even to Malindang itself, highlighting its global ecological importance.

Water Source

The range serves as a vital watershed for the provinces of Misamis Occidental, Zamboanga del Norte, and Zamboanga del Sur.

Cultural Heritage

The mountain is the ancestral domain of the Subanen people, whose traditional knowledge is essential to conservation efforts.

Biodiversity Hotspot

Home to hundreds of bird species, amphibians, and insects, Mount Malindang ranks among the Philippines' most biodiverse protected areas.

Tropical Climate Zones

Distinct elevation gradients create multiple microclimates, from warm lowlands to cool misty peaks, each supporting specialized species.

Threat & Conservation

Deforestation and climate change pose significant threats. Active protection and community engagement are vital to preserve this unique ecosystem.

🤝 Conservation Efforts

Active community-based and governmental programs are crucial for the long-term sustainability of the park.

  • Anti-Poaching Patrols: Organized teams monitor the range to prevent illegal hunting and logging.
  • Reforestation Projects: Focusing on planting endemic species to restore degraded areas, particularly in the buffer zones.
  • Community Education: Programs to engage local communities and indigenous groups in biodiversity monitoring and protection.
  • Ecotourism Regulation: Developing sustainable tourism practices to benefit locals while minimizing environmental impact.
  • Collaboration forged between Department of Environment and Natural Resources and Misamis University for Marine Ecosystem and Coastal Biodiversity Protection
  • Misamis University Established Two Hectares Permanent Monitoring Plot in the Southern Part of Mt. Malindang Range Natural Park in Collaboration with DENR 10.
  • Misamis University was contracted by the DENR-MMRNP PAMB to identify a 60 km Management Zone inside Mt. Malindang Range Natural Park, Misamis Occidental.
  • DENR and ASEAN Centre for Biodiversity (ACB) has partnered with Misamis University in advancing for effective protected area management: The MMRNP Protected Area Management Plan 2022-2032
